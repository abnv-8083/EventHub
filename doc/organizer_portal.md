# EventHub Organizer Portal

| Page Name | Purpose | UI Components | Functionality |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Org Dashboard** (`dashboard.html`) | Business performance overview. | Revenue charts, attendee counts, ticket sold-out alerts. | Tracking event success and daily performance. |
| **Create Event** (`create-event.html`) | Launching new experiences. | Multi-section form, banner upload, dynamic ticketing (+) icon. | Drafting and submitting events for platform listing. |
| **Manage Events** (`manage-events.html`) | Catalog control. | Event status table, edit/delete actions, search. | Controlling the lifecycle of events (publish, pause, end). |
| **Attendee List** (`bookings.html`) | On-ground management. | Filterable table, attendee profile modal, CSV export. | Checking who is coming and reviewing user profiles. |
| **Refunds** (`refunds.html`) | Handing cancellations. | Request list, approve/reject buttons, reason panel. | Reviewing and acting on attendee refund requests. |
| **Revenue** (`revenue.html`) | Financial reporting. | Earnings breakdown, payout status, tax summaries. | Tracking income and requesting payouts from the platform. |
| **Org Profile** (`profile.html`) | Brand management. | Company logo, team details, social proof links. | Managing the public-facing brand of the organizer. |
| **Ticket Scanner** (`scanner.html`) | On-site check-in. | Camera viewfinder, success sound cues, attendee validation. | Scanning QR codes and marking attendees as checked-in. |
